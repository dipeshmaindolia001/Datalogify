question: |
  Write a SQL query to find all employees who do not have a department assigned to them.
sampleData: |
  `Employee` table:
  | id | name | department_id |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | Alice | 10 |
  | 2 | Bob | NULL |
  | 3 | Charlie | 20 |
  
  `Department` table:
  | department_id | department_name |
  |---|---|
  | 10 | Engineering |
  | 20 | Sales |
answer: |
  ```sql
  SELECT e.*
  FROM Employee e
  LEFT JOIN Department d ON e.department_id = d.department_id
  WHERE d.department_id IS NULL;
  ```
explanation: |
  - Performing a `LEFT JOIN` retains all records from the `Employee` table.
  - If an employee does not have a matching department ID, columns from the `Department` table will evaluate to `NULL`.
  - Filtering for `WHERE d.department_id IS NULL` retrieves only those unassigned employees.
