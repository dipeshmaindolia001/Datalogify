question: |
  Write a SQL query to verify employees who joined after their assigned department was created.
sampleData: |
  `employees` table: `id`, `department_id`, `hire_date`
  `departments` table: `department_id`, `creation_date`
answer: |
  ```sql
  SELECT e.*
  FROM employees e
  JOIN departments d ON e.department_id = d.department_id
  WHERE e.hire_date > d.creation_date;
  ```
explanation: |
  - Join employee and department tables and compare timestamps.
