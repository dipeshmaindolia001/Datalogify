question: |
  Write a SQL query to find employees who have been with the company for over 10 full years.
sampleData: |
  `employees` table: `id`, `name`, `hire_date`
answer: |
  ```sql
  SELECT *
  FROM employees
  WHERE CURRENT_DATE - hire_date > INTERVAL '10 years';
  ```
explanation: |
  - Computes elapsed time since hire date and filters for $> 10$ years.
