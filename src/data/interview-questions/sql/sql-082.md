question: |
  Write a SQL query to find pairs of employees who joined the company in the exact same month and year.
sampleData: |
  `employees` table: `id`, `hire_date`
answer: |
  ```sql
  SELECT e1.id AS emp1_id, e2.id AS emp2_id, e1.hire_date
  FROM employees e1
  JOIN employees e2 ON e1.id < e2.id
   AND EXTRACT(MONTH FROM e1.hire_date) = EXTRACT(MONTH FROM e2.hire_date)
   AND EXTRACT(YEAR FROM e1.hire_date) = EXTRACT(YEAR FROM e2.hire_date);
  ```
explanation: |
  - Self-joins employee table on matching month & year while enforcing `e1.id < e2.id`.
